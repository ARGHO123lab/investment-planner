💳 What is an EMI?

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan. Each EMI consists of two components:

  • Principal repayment (the actual loan amount)
  • Interest charged by the lender (cost of borrowing)

This calculator helps you estimate your monthly repayment so you can plan your finances before applying for a loan. Knowing your EMI helps you understand your financial obligations and make informed borrowing decisions.

📊 Understanding Your EMI Result

Your EMI calculation depends on three key factors:

₹ Loan Amount

The principal amount you borrow. Higher loan amounts result in higher EMIs.

% Interest Rate

The annual interest rate charged by the lender. Higher rates increase your EMI and total interest paid.

📅 Loan Tenure

The repayment period in years. Longer tenures reduce monthly EMI but increase total interest.

🧮 How is EMI Calculated?

The EMI is calculated using a standard financial formula that takes into account your loan amount, interest rate, and tenure. The first few EMIs consist of more interest and less principal, while later EMIs have more principal repayment.

Example: A ₹40 lakh home loan at 8.5% interest for 20 years equals approximately ₹38,789 per month. Over 20 years, you'll pay ₹93.09 lakh in total (including ₹53.09 lakh as interest).

💡 Smart Borrowing Tips

  • Choose the shortest affordable tenure: Shorter tenures mean less interest paid overall.
  • Maintain a healthy credit score: Better scores qualify you for lower interest rates.
  • Compare lenders before borrowing: Different banks offer different interest rates and terms.
  • Avoid borrowing beyond repayment capacity: Ensure EMI is within 40-50% of your monthly income.
  • Make prepayments when possible: Reduce loan tenure and save on total interest.
  • Read loan agreement carefully: Understand all charges, penalties, and terms before signing.
  • Build emergency fund alongside EMI: Keep 6 months of expenses saved for emergencies.

📋 How to Use This EMI Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount you plan to borrow (in rupees).
  2. Enter the annual interest rate offered by your lender (usually between 5-12%).
  3. Enter the loan tenure in years (commonly 5-30 years depending on loan type).
  4. Click the Calculate EMI button.

  6. Adjust the values to compare different scenarios and find the best option for your situation.

🏦 EMI for Different Types of Loans

Home Loan

Interest Rate: 7-9%, Tenure: 15-30 years. Largest EMI due to high principal. Tax benefits available.

Car Loan

Interest Rate: 8-12%, Tenure: 3-7 years. Moderate EMI with vehicle as collateral. Insurance required.

Education Loan

Interest Rate: 6-10%, Tenure: 5-15 years. EMI starts after course completion. Some tax deductions available.

Personal Loan

Interest Rate: 10-18%, Tenure: 2-5 years. Highest interest rates but quick approval. No collateral needed.
