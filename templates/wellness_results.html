Understanding Your Results

Your scores are intended to help you identify areas of your financial habits or money knowledge that may deserve more attention.

A financial wellness score is not a measure of your personal worth or a guarantee of future financial outcomes. Financial circumstances can change over time as income, expenses, debt, savings, investments and financial goals change.

Use this report as a starting point for learning and planning, rather than as a professional financial assessment.
