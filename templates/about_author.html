About Argho Sanyal

I am Argho Sanyal, a software engineer and the founder of SmartPlanFinance.

I created SmartPlanFinance with a simple objective: to make personal finance more understandable, practical, and accessible to everyday people.

Money decisions often involve complicated concepts such as investing, compounding, inflation, taxes, loans, retirement planning, savings, and risk. SmartPlanFinance brings these concepts together through educational content, interactive calculators, and financial planning tools.

My Professional Background

My professional background is in software engineering and technology.

Working in technology has strongly influenced the way I approach financial education. I believe complicated problems become easier to understand when they are broken down into smaller components, assumptions are clearly explained, and the underlying calculations are made visible.

That philosophy is reflected throughout SmartPlanFinance. Instead of simply presenting a financial number, the platform aims to explain where the number comes from, which assumptions influence it, and what limitations should be considered.

Why I Created SmartPlanFinance

Personal finance can sometimes appear more complicated than it needs to be.

People often have practical questions such as: How much should I save every month? How much could my investments potentially grow? How does inflation affect future purchasing power? How much might I need for retirement? How should I think about my financial goals?

These questions become easier to explore when financial concepts are connected to understandable examples and actual numbers.

SmartPlanFinance was created to provide that practical bridge between financial concepts and everyday decisions.

My Approach to Personal Finance

Understand Before You Invest

Financial products can be useful, but understanding their purpose, risks, costs, assumptions, and time horizon should come before making a decision.

Numbers Need Context

A projected return or future value is meaningful only when the assumptions behind the calculation are clearly understood.

Think Long Term

Personal finance is generally a long-term process. Saving behaviour, consistency, risk management, and realistic expectations are important.

Keep Finance Practical

Financial education should not require a finance degree. Explanations and tools should be accessible to people at different stages of their financial journey.

How We Approach Financial Calculations

Financial calculators can produce impressive-looking numbers, but the number itself is not the complete answer.

Wherever practical, SmartPlanFinance aims to explain the methodology behind a calculation, including relevant formulas, assumptions, contribution frequency, investment period, estimated return, and other variables.

Calculator results are estimates based on the information entered by the user and the assumptions used by the particular calculator. Actual financial outcomes can differ significantly.

Editorial Principles

Financial information can influence real-world decisions. SmartPlanFinance therefore aims to approach financial content carefully and transparently.

Content should be understandable, calculations should be explainable, assumptions should be disclosed, and financial claims should not be presented as guaranteed outcomes.

When financial products, tax rules, regulations, interest rates, or other time-sensitive information are discussed, readers should verify the latest information with the relevant official source or a qualified professional before making a decision.
