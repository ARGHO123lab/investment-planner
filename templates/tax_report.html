💡 The Section 87A Rebate Advantage

For Assessment Year 2026–27, eligible resident individuals under the New Tax Regime may claim a rebate under Section 87A where their total income does not exceed ₹12,00,000. The maximum rebate is ₹60,000. For eligible salaried taxpayers, the standard deduction can also affect the income level at which the rebate becomes relevant. The rebate reduces the income-tax liability, subject to the applicable provisions and conditions. It is not a deduction from taxable income.

The calculator provides an estimate using the assumptions and tax rules incorporated into SmartPlan Finance's calculation logic. Income-tax liability can also depend on applicable rebates, deductions, exemptions, surcharge, marginal relief, cess, income type and other individual circumstances.
